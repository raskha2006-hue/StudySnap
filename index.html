      How it works

      01

      Upload a photo

      The photo is shrunk in your browser (max 1600px) and then sent to the server. Nothing is saved.

      02

      Gemma 4 reads it

      The image and the instructions go to Gemma 4 in one request. It reads the handwriting, diagrams and formulas and replies with structured study material.

      03

      Practise

      Read the explanation, take the quiz, flip the flashcards, and ask the chat about anything that is still unclear.

      Where Gemma 4 is used

      The whole project runs on one open model, served through the Gemini API. Four places:

      • Understanding the image — /api/analyze sends the photo and the prompt to Gemma 4 together (multimodal input).
      • Structured output — a system instruction makes the model reply in a fixed JSON shape: explanation, quiz, flashcards.
      • Doubt chat — /api/chat runs a multi-turn chat session with the uploaded material as context.
      • Thinking mode — switching the toggle on sets thinking_level="high".

      Model: gemma-4-26b-a4b-it · full details are in readme.md and documents.md.
